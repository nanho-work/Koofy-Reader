Version 1.10.0 adds an on-device reading journal. Open Journal in the bottom navigation to see reading days, duration, books by date, and daily/monthly graphs. Screenshots use illustrative local history rendered with the actual app UI. No sign-in is required. Favorites select the works eligible for new-story alerts; OS notification permission is requested at first reading and can be declined. Notification registration now waits for APNs readiness. The catalog keeps cached data and uses a 30-minute freshness window, with scheduled-publication refreshes. Banners remain only in the reader. Optional rewarded ads hide banners for 4 hours; no paid or ad-unlocked early-access chapters are included. Analytics starts off; required UMP consent remains and ATT is not requested automatically. Korean, English and Japanese are supported.
