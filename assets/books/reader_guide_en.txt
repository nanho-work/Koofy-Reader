[장] Getting started with Koofy Reader

1. Add a book
Tap “Add books” in your library and choose a TXT or EPUB file.

2. Open your book
Tap its cover in the library.

3. Make reading comfortable
Open “Reading settings” to change the text size and background.

Need help with another feature?
Open the table of contents to jump to a topic.
The detailed help at the end explains limits and exceptions.

[장] App language and this guide

Use the globe button at the top right of the library to choose 한국어, English or 日本語.
Initially, the app follows your device language. Unsupported languages fall back to English.
A language you select remains selected after restarting the app.
Choose “Device language” to return to automatic selection.

This guide’s title, cover, contents and text follow the selected language.
Ordinary book titles and text are not translated or changed.
The app language does not change the listening voice or the English → Korean translation direction.

Each language has different text, so reading positions are stored separately.
The first time you open another language version, a content-change notice may appear.
Returning to the original language restores that version’s saved position.

[장] Add books from your files

1. Tap “Add books” in the library.

2. Choose TXT or EPUB files in your device’s file picker.
You can select several files together.

3. When importing finishes, tap a book’s cover to read it.

You can organize multiple books into a collection.
Choose to keep them separately if you prefer individual books.

If Korean characters look garbled, check the text preview.
See “Help · Importing files” for details.

[장] Download available books

1. Open “Books & fonts” in the library.

2. Under “Books”, search by title or author.
You can also narrow the list by category.

3. Tap the download icon for a standalone book.
A check mark appears when it is added to your library.

Tap a title to see its description, source and terms of use.
Downloading requires internet access.
Once downloaded, books are available offline.

[장] Read a series and its next episode

1. Select a series in the book download list.

2. Find an episode inside the series.
Use the episode number or title search to find it.

3. Download the episode, then open it in your library.

New episodes appear after they are published.
To organize downloaded episodes together, create a book collection.

[장] Find books in your library

To keep reading
Tap “Continue reading” in the library.
Each book resumes from its own saved position.

To find another book
Enter its title or author in the search field.
Use filters and sorting to narrow the list.

If a book is missing
Clear the search and filters, then pull down to refresh the library.

[장] Use the reading screen

Turn a page
Tap a screen edge or the previous and next buttons.

Hide or show reading controls
Tap the center of the text.

Go to another chapter
Choose its title in the table of contents.

With the page-turn effect enabled
You can also press an edge and drag to turn the page.

[장] Change text and background

1. Open a book and tap “Reading settings”.

2. Under “Display”, choose the text size and background.
You can also change the reading mode and page layout.

3. Expand the font and paragraph settings to adjust spacing.
Change line spacing, paragraph spacing, margins and font.

Appearance settings apply across books.
Reading positions are saved separately for each book.

[장] Download and apply fonts

1. Open “Books & fonts” in the library.

2. Under “Fonts”, download a font you like.

3. Open a book, then choose “Display” in “Reading settings”.
Select the downloaded font in the font and paragraph settings.

If a new font is missing, return to the library and reopen the book.
For your own font files, see “Help · Add personal fonts”.

[장] Change a book cover

1. Tap “…” at the top right of a book card.

2. Choose the option to add or change its cover image.

3. Select a JPG, PNG or WebP image.

Changing the cover keeps the text and reading position intact.
Resetting the cover restores its default appearance.
Bundled books return to their included cover.

[장] Add several covers at once

1. Match the book and cover filenames.
Example: novel_001.txt / novel_001.jpg

2. Select books and images together when adding books.
For books already in the library, use the batch cover option.

3. Check the matches and apply them.
Enable replacement of existing covers if you want to overwrite them.

Collections also have a batch cover option for their books.
Matching uses original filenames, not the displayed book titles.

[장] Organize books into collections

1. Open a book’s “…” menu and choose to create a collection.

2. Select books, then check the collection title and order.

3. Open the saved collection and choose a book to read.

You can also hold a book and drag it onto another book.
To use an existing collection, choose the option to add to a collection.

A collection gathers books in one place.
Each book keeps its own text and reading records.

[장] Collection order and the next book

Change the order
Open the collection and move its reorder handles.
You can also sort by title and episode number.

Read the next book
Check the title under “Next book” on the reading screen, then tap “Open”.
You can also continue from the last page.

The next book follows the collection’s order.
It does not open automatically. Previously read books resume from their saved position.

[장] Delete or empty a collection

Remove a collection and its books
Open the collection’s “…” menu and choose to delete it.
The collection and its books move to the trash together.

Keep an empty collection
Open the collection, then choose to empty it in the “…” menu.
Only its books move to the trash.

Keep the books but remove the grouping
Ungroup the collection. Its books return to the library.
To remove just one book from a collection, use its remove-from-collection option.

[장] Delete and restore books

1. Choose delete in a book’s “…” menu.
The book moves to the trash instead of being erased immediately.

2. To read it again, open “Settings” in the library.

3. Open “Trash” and tap “Restore” for the item.

Files, covers and reading records remain stored while an item is in the trash.
Books in the trash are excluded from library backups.

[장] Empty the trash to free space

1. Open “Trash” from the library’s “Settings”.

2. Permanently delete an individual item, or empty the trash to delete all items.

3. Check the items before confirming.

Important: permanently deleted items cannot be restored.
Restore or back up books you need first.

Originals stored outside the app and files shared with other books are kept.
Built-in source files for bundled books are not removed.

[장] Search text and use bookmarks

Find text
Tap the search control or magnifying glass at the top of the reading screen.
Enter a word or phrase in the text search and select a result.

Mark a place
Add a bookmark at your current position and give it a name.
Use the bookmark list to return to saved places.

Return to where you were
Tap the back-to-position arrow at the bottom.
It is available after jumping through the contents, search results or bookmarks.

[장] Listen to a book

1. Choose “Listen” in “Reading settings”.

2. Start or resume listening.
The device voice reads from the visible position.

3. Tap Ⅱ to pause.
Ending listening hides the playback controls.

Change the voice, speed and sleep timer in listening settings.

Listening pauses when you leave the app or lock the screen.
It does not resume automatically when you return.
The current listening feature uses local Korean voices; changing the app language does not change the voice language.

[장] Translate English passages

1. Enable translation mode under “Translate” in “Reading settings”.

2. First, prepare translation over Wi-Fi to download the language data.

3. Hold the text and adjust the handles to select a word or passage.
The translation panel shows the English original and Korean translation.

Turn translation off from the panel when finished.
After the language data is ready, translation works offline.

See “Help · Translation” for limits and data handling.
Changing the app language does not change the English-to-Korean translation direction.

[장] Back up your library

1. Open library backup and restore from the library’s “Settings”.

2. Choose to save a backup file.

3. Pick a location outside the app in the file picker.
The backup is saved as one .koofy.zip file.

It includes books, covers, personal fonts, collections, reading records and settings.
Restore books from the trash before backing up if you want to include them.

Before deleting the app, check that the backup is safely stored.
This is not automatic synchronization between devices.

[장] Restore a backup

1. Transfer the backup file to the device where you want to use it.

2. Open library backup and restore in “Settings”.

3. Select the backup file and restore it.

Books and records that are missing on the current device are added.
Existing records for the same book and existing collection order are kept.

On a new device, download catalog fonts and device voices again.
The detailed help explains what is included and the size limits.

[장] Temporarily hide ads

1. Open “Settings” at the top of the library.

2. Choose to watch an ad for two hours of reading without ads.

3. Finish watching and check that the reward was applied.
Banners in the library, settings and reading screen are hidden for two hours.

Closing the ad early may prevent the reward.
The time continues to pass while the app is closed.
Check the remaining time in settings.

[장] If something goes wrong

1. Open diagnostic information for reporting a problem in the library’s settings.

2. Copy the diagnostics.
They do not include book text, titles or file paths.

3. Tell us the steps that led to the problem and the error message.
The file format and whether the screen was folded are also helpful.

Contact: koofylab@gmail.com
Diagnostics and book text are not sent automatically.

[장] Help · Importing files

Supported: TXT and ordinary text-focused EPUB without DRM.
PDF, comic-specific formats, DRM-protected ebooks and fixed-layout EPUB are not supported.

TXT supports UTF-8 and UTF-16 with a BOM.
Korean CP949/EUC-KR text can be imported after checking the preview.
Only the app’s copy is converted; the original is unchanged.

Books with identical content are not added twice, even if filenames differ.
If the content changes under the same filename, the old book is kept and a new one is added.

If some files fail, importing continues for the others.
Check the failed filenames after importing finishes.
How to select several files depends on your device’s file picker.

[장] Help · Covers and collections

Batch covers match original filenames without their extensions.
English letter case and spaces at either end are ignored.
Choose the correct match if filenames overlap.
For older backups without original filenames, add covers individually.

Enable the cover option in settings to show a registered cover as the first page.
A collection’s representative image is not used as each book’s first page.
An EPUB’s existing cover is not added twice.

Resuming starts in the text without showing the cover again.
You can return to the cover through the contents; listening skips it.

Use the collection’s cover display option to show its books’ covers.
Finished counts follow the marks you set yourself.
Reaching the last page does not automatically mark a book finished.

[장] Help · Create a TXT table of contents

Write a chapter title line as “[장] Title”.
Put [장] at the very start of the line, followed by a space or tab and the title.
The marker remains [장] in every app language.

Example: write “[장] Episode 1” on a title line and the text on the next line.
Use “[장] Episode 2” for the next episode’s title line.

The reading screen hides the marker and adds the title to the contents.
In paged reading, each chapter begins on a new page.
Long chapters can continue across several pages.

Markers without a title or preceded by spaces are not recognized.
Blank lines alone do not create chapters.
Continuous scrolling displays the chapters in sequence.

The same rules apply to imported and downloaded TXT files.
EPUB uses the table of contents included in the file.

[장] Help · Reading screen and records

Two-page layout is available when the text area is wide enough.
Folding the screen switches to one page; widening it restores your selection.
A new chapter does not always start on the left page.

Default line and paragraph spacing follows the book’s own styles.
Changing paragraph spacing does not remove blank lines in the source.
Page layout and transition effects cannot be adjusted in continuous scrolling.

Page numbers can change with text size, font or screen layout.
Your position is restored using the text you were reading.

When the text itself changes, the old position is not applied directly.
Previous records are preserved. Use the new contents to find your place.
This can also happen when this guide is revised or you open another language version.
Each language version has its own reading position; returning to it restores that version’s saved position.

Books and records are stored on the device.
Back up before deleting the app or clearing its data. Automatic sync is not supported.

[장] Help · Search and bookmarks

Text search works within the current book and does not need internet access.
Up to 500 results are shown at a time. Narrow your query if there are too many.
Library search finds book titles and authors.

You can save up to 100 bookmarks per book; they are included in library backups.
On iOS, swipe an item in the list to delete it.
On Android, tap an item and choose delete.

The return history keeps the latest 20 jumps.
It resets when you close the book.
Old bookmarks are not automatically applied when the text changes.

[장] Help · Add personal fonts

Open the font and paragraph controls under Reading settings → Display.
Choose the personal font management option and select TTF or OTF files.

Each file must be no larger than 10MB; you can keep up to 50 fonts.
The same file is not added twice.
After adding a font, return to reading to reopen the book at its saved position.

Fonts installed in device settings are not all imported automatically.
Choose font files you have saved yourself.

The app keeps a copy, so moving the original does not remove the font.
Personal fonts are included in backups. Deleting the active font restores the default font.

[장] Help · Listening

Listening uses local Korean voices installed on your device.
Book text is not sent to a server for speech synthesis.
Available voices and their quality vary by device.

On Android, use the device voice download option if no voice is available.
On iPhone, install voices through Accessibility in device settings.
Reopen the reading screen after installation.

Hold the play button to change the voice, speed and timer.
You can also keep the listening button visible and follow the voice with page turns.

Leaving the app, locking the screen, calls, unplugging headphones and tapping an ad pause listening.
A banner simply being visible does not pause it.
Jumping to another page manually pauses playback; the next playback starts at the new position.

The listening position is saved separately from the reading position.
Use the saved listening position option to continue listening.
Playback stops at the end of the book and does not automatically open the next book in a collection.

[장] Help · Translation

Translation is English → Korean, with up to 2,000 selected characters at a time.
Scroll inside the translation panel for long results.
Vocabulary lists and grammar explanations are not currently provided.

Listen to the selected original or translation using the respective controls.
A device voice for that language is required; playback does not start automatically.
Your existing reading and listening positions are unchanged.

The app uses Google Translate’s on-device translation.
Selected text and translations are not sent to a translation server.
The SDK may communicate with Google to download language models and report performance and usage statistics.

Translations are for learning reference and may not accurately reflect context or idioms.

[장] Help · Backup scope and limits

Backups include original book files, covers, personal fonts, collections and their order.
Reading positions, bookmarks, finished marks, appearance and cover display settings are included.
Voice settings and listening positions are included, but device voice files and sleep timers are not.

When restoring, current records and settings on the device take priority.
A new device receives the backup’s appearance settings.
Choose a voice again when moving between Android and iPhone.

Download catalog fonts again on the new device.
Ad-free time and ad consent preferences are not backed up.

Up to 100MB of original files, covers and related data can be backed up at once.
You will see a message if an original is missing or a backup is damaged.
If restoring fails, you can try the same file again.

Backups contain book text. Store them somewhere you control.

[장] Help · Privacy and ads

Change your ad preference in Settings → Privacy and ads in the library.
You can also read the privacy policy offline.
Reading is available regardless of your ad choice.

Personalized ads are subject to separate consent and applicable iOS tracking permission.
You can report inappropriate ads from the same menu.
Book text and reading records are not attached automatically.

Rewarded ad time is applied when completion is confirmed.
The confirmation may arrive late.
You cannot accumulate time in advance; you can watch again after it expires.

If no ad appears, check your internet connection and try again later. Ad availability varies.
